Keep the beat

Set a tempo, or tap it in, and shape the bar with accents and subdivisions. The clicks are generated in your browser and timed by the audio clock, so they stay steady.

Accents

Click a beat to change it: normal → accent → mute → soft. Shift-click goes the other way. Muting a beat also mutes its subdivisions.
