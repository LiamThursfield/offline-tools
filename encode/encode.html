Encode and decode text

Convert text to and from Base64, URL encoding, HTML entities, hex, string escapes and Unicode escapes, and see hidden characters. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Input

empty
Alphabet Lines Standard and URL-safe Base64, with or without padding and line breaks, and data: URLs. Encode as Escape Named (&eacute;), decimal (&#233;) and hex (&#xE9;) references. Separator Spaces, colons, commas, 0x and \x are ignored. Hex dumps from xxd and hexdump -C work too. JSON and JavaScript escapes, with or without the surrounding quotes. Style \uXXXX, \u{…}, \U…, \xHH, U+XXXX, &#x…; and CSS escapes, mixed freely.
