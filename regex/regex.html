        Syntax

        Characters and classes

        .Any character except line breaks (including them with s)
        \d \DA digit 0–9, or anything else
        \w \WA word character (A–Z a–z 0–9 _), or anything else
        \s \SWhitespace (space, tab, line breaks, Unicode spaces), or anything else
        \t \n \rTab, line feed, carriage return
        \x41 \u0041A character by code; \u{1F600} with u or v
        \p{L} \P{L}A Unicode property, or not: \p{Lu}, \p{Script=Greek}, \p{Emoji} (u or v)
        \. \* \\A special character, literally
        [abc] [^abc]Any one of these, or none of them
        [a-z]Any character in a range
        [\w--\d]Set difference, and && for intersection (v only)

        Groups and lookaround

        (abc)Capture group, numbered from 1 by its opening bracket
        (?<name>abc)Named capture group
        (?:abc)Group without capturing
        \1 \k<name>Backreference: the same text a group matched
        a|bEither side
        (?=abc)Followed by, without including it
        (?!abc)Not followed by
        (?<=abc)Preceded by
        (?<!abc)Not preceded by
        (?i:abc)Flags i, m or s for part of the pattern (newer browsers)

        Quantifiers and anchors

        * + ?0 or more, 1 or more, 0 or 1
        {3} {2,} {2,5}Exactly 3, 2 or more, 2 to 5
        *? +? {2,5}?Lazy: as few as possible instead of as many
        ^ $Start and end of the text, or of each line with m
        \b \BA word boundary, or not one

        Replacement

        $1 … $99What a group matched (empty if it didn’t take part)
        $<name>What a named group matched
        $&The whole match
        $` $'The text before and after the match
        $$A literal $

        Anything else, such as $0 or $5 when there are only 4 groups, is kept as it is.
